Rent Ride - Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about car rentals below.

1. How do I book a car rental? +

Booking a car rental is simple. You can book online through our website or contact our customer service for assistance.

2. What documents do I need to rent a car? +

To rent a car, you typically need a valid driver's license, a credit card for payment, and sometimes an additional form of identification.

3. Are there age restrictions for renting a car? +

Yes, rental companies often require drivers to be at least 21 years old. Additional fees may apply for drivers under 25.

4. Can I modify or cancel my car reservation? +

Yes, you can usually modify or cancel your reservation online or by contacting our customer support team.

5. What if the car breaks down during my rental period? +

If the car breaks down, contact our roadside assistance immediately. We will arrange for assistance to get you back on track.
